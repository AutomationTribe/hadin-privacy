# Privacy Policy for Hadin

**Effective date:** 24 September 2026

Intello Technologies (“Hadin,” “we,” “our,” or “us”) provides the Hadin mobile application, a personal travel-safety service that helps users track journeys, share safety updates with trusted contacts, and activate SOS features.

This Privacy Policy explains what information Hadin collects, why it is collected, how it is used and shared, and the choices available to users.

## 1. Information We Collect

### Account information

When you create or manage an account, we may collect:

- Your name
- Email address
- Telephone number
- Authentication information
- Account and subscription status
- Google account information when you choose Google sign-in

Passwords and authentication credentials are handled through our authentication provider and are not stored by Hadin in plain text.

### Location and journey information

When you activate trip tracking, live-location sharing, always-on safety features, or an SOS, we may collect:

- Precise or approximate location
- Location timestamps
- Location accuracy, speed and direction
- Trip origin and destination
- Expected arrival time
- Trip status and history
- Device battery or connectivity information needed to maintain tracking

Location is collected only after the relevant device permission is granted. Some location information may be temporarily stored on your device when it cannot immediately be transmitted.

### Trusted-contact information

When you create a safety circle, we may collect information you provide about trusted contacts, including:

- Name
- Telephone number
- Email address, where provided
- Relationship to you
- Contact identifiers used for trip and SOS notifications

You should only provide another person’s information when you have permission to do so.

### SOS and safety-event information

When you activate an SOS or related safety feature, we may collect:

- SOS activation and cancellation times
- Your location during the incident
- Contacts selected to receive the alert
- Notification and communication logs
- Safety-event history
- Audio recordings when you grant microphone permission and activate a feature that records audio

SOS audio may be recorded in segments and uploaded to private cloud storage. Hadin does not record audio unless the relevant safety feature is active and microphone permission has been granted.

### Payment and subscription information

If you purchase a subscription, payment processing is handled by Paystack or another payment provider displayed during checkout. We may receive:

- Transaction reference
- Payment status
- Subscription plan
- Payment date and amount
- Limited billing information supplied by the payment provider

Hadin does not directly store complete payment-card numbers or card security codes.

### Device and technical information

We may collect limited technical information required to operate and secure the service, including:

- Device and operating-system information
- App version
- Push-notification token
- Network and connectivity status
- Diagnostic information and error logs
- Security and authentication records

### Information stored on your device

Hadin may store session information, preferences, queued location updates and feature settings locally on your device. Some locally stored information is cleared when you sign out or remove the app.

## 2. How We Use Information

We use information to:

- Create and authenticate accounts
- Provide journey tracking and safety features
- Share authorised safety updates with trusted contacts
- Send trip, SOS and account notifications
- Detect missed arrivals or unusual trip events
- Store and present trip and safety-event history
- Process subscriptions and verify payments
- Provide customer support
- Maintain security and prevent abuse
- Diagnose failures and improve reliability
- Comply with legal and regulatory obligations

We do not sell personal information.

## 3. How Information Is Shared

### Trusted contacts

When you start a trip, share your location, or activate an SOS, information may be sent to the trusted contacts you selected. This may include your name, current location, trip information and safety-event details.

### Service providers

We use service providers to operate Hadin. Depending on the features you use, these may include:

- Supabase for authentication, databases and private file storage
- Paystack for payment processing
- Google services for sign-in, maps and location-related functionality
- Push-notification providers
- Email, SMS and telecommunications providers used to deliver safety alerts
- Hosting, infrastructure, security and support providers

These providers process information only as necessary to provide their services and are subject to their own privacy terms.

### Legal and safety purposes

We may disclose information when reasonably necessary to:

- Comply with applicable law, regulation, legal process or government requests
- Protect a person from serious harm
- Investigate fraud, abuse or security incidents
- Protect the rights, property or safety of Hadin, its users or others

### Business transfers

If Hadin is involved in a merger, acquisition, reorganisation or sale of assets, information may be transferred as part of that transaction, subject to applicable law.

## 4. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide Hadin, meet legal obligations, resolve disputes and enforce agreements.

Our standard retention periods are:

- Account information: while the account remains active and for up to 60 days after account deletion
- Trip and location history: up to 30 days
- SOS-event information: up to 60 days
- SOS audio recordings: up to 60 days
- Security logs and backups: up to 90 days
- Payment and transaction records: for the period required by applicable financial and tax laws

Information may remain temporarily in protected backups after deletion until the normal backup-retention period expires.

We may retain particular records for longer when required by law, needed to resolve a dispute, or reasonably necessary to investigate fraud, abuse or a security incident.

## 5. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational safeguards designed to protect personal information, including access controls, authenticated service requests, secure data transmission, private cloud-storage permissions and restricted database access.

No electronic storage or transmission method is completely secure. We therefore cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 6. Your Choices and Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

- Access personal information held about you
- Correct inaccurate information
- Request deletion of your account and data
- Object to or restrict certain processing
- Withdraw consent
- Request a portable copy of your information
- Complain to an appropriate data-protection authority

You may control location, microphone and notification permissions through your device settings. Disabling a permission may prevent the corresponding Hadin feature from working.

To make a privacy request, contact [hello@intellotechnologies.com](mailto:hello@intellotechnologies.com).

### Account deletion

You can request deletion through Hadin’s account-deletion feature or by visiting:

[https://intellotechnologies.com/hadin/delete](https://intellotechnologies.com/hadin/delete)

After a valid deletion request is completed, account information may be retained for up to 60 days, subject to legal requirements. Information in protected backups may remain for up to 90 days before being overwritten through the normal backup cycle.

## 7. Children’s Privacy

Hadin is not directed to children under 13 or below the minimum legal age required in the user’s country.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children without legally required consent. If you believe a child has provided personal information improperly, contact us so that we can investigate and delete it where appropriate.

## 8. International Data Transfers

Hadin’s service providers may process information in countries other than the country where you live. Where required, we use appropriate safeguards for international transfers of personal information.

## 9. Third-Party Services

Hadin may contain links to or integrate with third-party services. Their handling of information is governed by their respective privacy policies. We encourage users to review those policies.

## 10. Emergency-Service Disclaimer

Hadin is intended to support personal safety and communication with trusted contacts. It is not a replacement for police, ambulance, fire, medical or other emergency services.

Hadin cannot guarantee that a trusted contact will receive or respond to an alert. Delivery may be affected by network availability, device settings, battery level and third-party services.

## 11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when Hadin’s features, practices or legal obligations change.

The revised policy will be published at the same URL with an updated effective date. Where required by law, we will provide additional notice or request consent.
